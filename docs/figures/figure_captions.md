## Layout & typography

- Full-width figures: 7.2 in (183 mm); panel labels: 9 pt bold; all other text: 8 pt.
- Sans-serif font (DejaVu Sans / Arial), 0.5 pt axes, inward ticks, diverging maps use the coolwarm colormap.
- PNG is 300 dpi for slides/Word; PDF is vector for print.

## Simulation parameters (edit at the top of the script)

- Image 640 x 640 px, aperture radius 240 px
- Carrier f0 = 1/16 cyc/px (fringe period 16 px)
- Gaussian bandpass sigma = 12 bins, noise amplitude 0.012
- Surfaces: tilt + defocus + 0-degree astigmatism (test); small residual (reference)
